# Research lineage and negative outcomes

This index covers every related local output directory present at export: **41 stages**, including completed studies, budget stops and unexecuted proposals. It is a historical evidence archive, not 41 independent replications and not a single prospectively registered research program. Each stage has its own frozen scope.

## Model identities and evidence progression

1. **DEND/POINT state mechanisms:** the early `TAN-` directory prefix is historical. These circuits did not implement original TAN temporal attention. State interventions, restricted retraining, width and learning-rate studies have distinct causal scopes.
2. **Original TAN and DEND branch attention:** the original-cell population pilot failed through output silence. The subsequent DEND-plus-history-injection model retained direct continuous integration and did not replace its cells with whole TAN cells. Its null/negative attention comparisons are retained.
3. **Readout diagnosis and attention controls:** frozen probes, state transfer and timing diagnostics used the same original three training blocks. Probe decoding, forced tie-breaking, strict native accuracy and coverage are distinct outcomes. Later two-scale attention confirmation did not meet its joint hypothesis.
4. **Addressed memory and interference:** discrete address selection, norm-controlled interventions and learned continuous-key matching established bounded routing effects. Capacity and cross-state interventions reused trained weights. Reset and fixed warm-state candidates failed their adoption criterion before visible-event replay was developed.
5. **Independent confirmations:** replay was confirmed on new training blocks and four held-out schedules. The new evicted-key veto was first explored on those weights, then confirmed using another fresh set of 12 training blocks. These estimates are not pooled.
6. **Execution engineering:** equivalence fixtures, failed budgets, batching and archive-format measurements enabled the final bounded run. They are not additional scientific accuracy replications or physical-energy experiments.

## Interpretation rules

The independent sampling unit is the paired training/data block, not an evaluation path, intervention, test example or audit check. Reused weights do not create fresh training replication. Later two-endpoint percentile-bootstrap intervals are nominal small-sample intervals; their multiplicity scope does not cover the whole adaptive exploration sequence. A locally frozen protocol is not a claim of external preregistration.

Native count ties are errors under strict accuracy, while forced binary probes break ties; coverage must remain separate. A zero empirical interval or 100% observed score is not a population certainty guarantee. State transplantation does not establish exclusive storage, universal necessity or attractor counts. The final veto restores a small legacy-replay loss and preserves recent-record gains; it does not recover missing values or increase memory capacity.

Counts of MACs, emitted spikes, delivered events, arrays and wall time are distinct. No joules, ATP or biological-time claims are supported. Shared execution time is counted once per group, not once per logical output. Isolated selected-call latency is descriptive. Independent equation implementations were within the same research workflow, not external peer-reviewed replication.
