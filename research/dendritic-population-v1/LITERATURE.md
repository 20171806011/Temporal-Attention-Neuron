# Primary literature and biological boundaries

These sources motivate a computational abstraction, not a claim that the implementation reproduces a biological circuit. Literature was checked before the experiment on 2026-11-03.

| Primary source | Kind of evidence and supported connection | What this prototype does not reproduce |
|---|---|---|
| Poirazi, Brannon & Mel (2003), *Pyramidal neuron as two-layer neural network*. Neuron37:989–999. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12670427/), DOI10.1016/S0896-6273(03)00149-1. | Detailed compartmental simulation of CA1 pyramidal neurons motivated nonlinear dendritic subunits followed by somatic combination, in a firing-rate abstraction. | Not a physiological demonstration that this six-cell network is a brain circuit; no morphology, membrane capacitance, channel kinetics or fitted biological parameters here. |
| Larkum, Zhu & Sakmann (1999), *A new cellular mechanism for coupling inputs arriving at different cortical layers*. Nature398:338–341. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10192334/), DOI10.1038/18686. | Primary experimental work on coupling inputs in pyramidal cells. Supports distinguishing dendritic and somatic locations. | We do not reproduce backpropagating action-potential/calcium coincidence mechanisms, infer their parameters, or remove native firing. Publisher full text was inaccessible during this review; only accessible indexed metadata/abstract information was used. |
| Gidon et al. (2020), *Dendritic action potentials and computation in human layer2/3 cortical neurons*. Science367:83–87. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31896716/), [publisher](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aax6239). | Ex vivo human cortical dendrite experiments motivate taking active dendrites seriously. | The publisher returned403; no full-text claims, detailed XOR reproduction, human-brain equivalence or channel calibration are asserted. Our graded dendrite variable is not a physiological dendritic spike. |
| Guerguiev, Lillicrap & Richards (2017), *Towards deep learning with segregated dendrites*. eLife6:e22901. [Primary full text](https://elifesciences.org/articles/22901), DOI10.7554/eLife.22901. | Computational learning model with segregated sensory/feedback compartments and local learning signals. A reason to investigate a learning extension later. | This first prototype has fixed weights, no apical feedback or plasticity, and no claim of biological credit assignment. |

The local rectifier is an engineering abstraction. Point-neuron circuits can also implement conjunctions; the state-matched ordinary spiking network is therefore a required strong comparator. A positive difference against global pooling alone would only demonstrate the constructed task's known need for separated nonlinear features.

No access control was bypassed and inaccessible publisher pages were not retried. No external paper text, figures, or third-party code is copied into the implementation.
