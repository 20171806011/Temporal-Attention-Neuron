## What is frozen here

The retained findings distinguish history-dependent dynamics, accessible information, permitted readout, and learnability. They establish particular static-addressing properties and particular response-map obstructions. They also record a training-composition effect and a noise-dependent detection tradeoff. Each finding retains its model, task, distribution, observation, and resource assumptions.

The four-part conceptual distinction is an organizing framework, not one newly proved universal theorem. The canonical paper separately distinguishes history sensitivity, addressing, association preservation, and observable recovery; the broader learnability distinction links to later population work without folding those experiments into this mainline paper.
