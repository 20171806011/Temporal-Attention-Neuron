## Verification boundaries retained explicitly

The manuscript's saved validation reports 774 source/snapshot/arithmetic checks, with no new simulation, training, or external replication. The clean-map verifier reports 5,482 checks of four exact-real collision brackets, reset branches, and 64 saved trajectories. Its own scope excludes independent regeneration of the local derivative rectangle and global monotonicity certificates, a finite-precision impossibility theorem, classifier training, and Bayes-risk estimation. A large number of passing checks does not erase those exclusions.

The archival copy includes existing AI-assisted internal verification. The words “independent checker” in historical filenames mean a separately implemented checker within that workflow, not an unaffiliated laboratory or human replication. Chat-sandbox conceptual experiments remain separately identified and are not promoted to locally replicated results.

## Safe closure statement

> Mainline A is archived as a bounded account of history sensitivity, static addressing, observable recoverability, and task-specific limitations in the recorded TAN implementations. Exact statements retain their mathematical contracts, empirical statements retain their tested conditions, and internal checks remain distinguished from external replication. The population-learning experiments form a separate research line.
