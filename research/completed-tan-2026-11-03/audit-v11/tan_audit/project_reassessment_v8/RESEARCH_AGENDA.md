The new result separates four claims often conflated: different trajectories, sufficient full state, stable storage under perturbations, and recovery through a specified observation. Natural collisions address the first two only in a projection-dependent sense. P1A establishes lookup with a granted candidate array. The new Probe1 check shows that even an explicit window containing the correct positional target can fail the native response contract. None supplies biological robustness or a universal memory-capacity theorem.

Source review found KSG outputs in natural-log units placed beside discrete bits, an inconsistent bias correction/bin mapping, and a same-time cascade that omits membrane history. The steady-noise spike-count endpoint also rewards an always-silent output. These are concrete measurement issues to verify with fixed calibration controls and original-trace reconstruction, not new information theory and not yet an executed correction. The saved Layer4 trace ledger was not found; the source and summary table are present. Details and primary references: BROAD_MANUSCRIPT_REVIEW.md.
2. **Keep the model's demonstrated role distinct from desired addressing.** Current original TAN-I evidence supports history-dependent saliency/deviation gating with explicit storage; it does not establish flexible content addressing or robust target recovery. A future recovery mechanism must specify how the requested position/key affects the readout and how useful order survives reset. Do not infer that merely removing reset, exposing another state, or enlarging a decoder solves the full task. Any changed operation needs its own resource contract and fresh test.
3. **Finish source fidelity before release.** The v7 manuscript/readme patches remain unapplied; full release layout is not rebuilt. Preserve the original results and distinguish implementation corrections from interpretive narrowing. H3 internal composition remains deferred; an explicit arithmetic combiner is not evidence of learned internal composition.

No further box widening, decoder fitting, query redesign, estimator sweep or causal intervention was run after the selected fixed study. The next measurement audit is a bounded agenda item, not a promise of an unobserved background run.
