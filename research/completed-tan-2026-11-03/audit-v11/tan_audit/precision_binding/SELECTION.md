# Why this next test, before execution

The prior result is the two-point case of standard polynomial interpolation, not a new memory theorem: the authoritative mathematical reference describes the unique polynomial through distinct nodes. [NIST DLMF3.3](https://dlmf.nist.gov/3.3#i). M0 already gives exact clean answers by retaining both key/value records. Another unquantized success would repeat that known task result.

Input-dependent fast-weight memories also store associations through explicit write/update operations; their controller/update rules are part of the mechanism, rather than consequences of merely choosing a small state dimension. [Schlag, Irie and Schmidhuber2021](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/schlag21a.html). Hopfield's recurrent content-addressable pattern-completion model is a different retrieval contract from exact valid-key two-point interpolation. [Hopfield1982](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC346238/). Neither source establishes that this two-register write is a biological neuron or supplies free phase control/division.

Selected question: at a single fixed persistent-bit budget, does counting precision during the first-event holding phase change the apparent benefit of the compact final coefficient representation? The earlier additive-noise experiment perturbed final coefficients and separately tested leaking first records; it did not impose a fixed end-to-end digital payload budget. First-event key error is amplified by the reciprocal key separation when the second event arrives. That missing intermediate precision is a concrete implementation resource.

We will compare two16-bit interpolation words with a four8-bit key/value cache, each with an explicit2-bit controller. One interpolation arm keeps fixed raw register scales; one explicitly switches scale interpretation with phase. An endpoint-only quantization diagnostic deliberately retains an unquantized first record and therefore is not budget-matched. There is one bit width, one prescribed range choice and no fitted quantizer or sweep. Analytic guardbands are derived before data to prevent the prior study's zero-headroom boundary failures from dominating the question.

This is a principled persistent-precision test, not a complete hardware/energy comparison or simulation of the registered neuronal ODE. Different range interpretation/gain switching, arithmetic precision, current input ports, holding time and output precision remain explicit. A time-bounded division circuit would require further dynamics and counted working state; inventing one implicitly would obscure rather than close the present gap. A general exact finite-time smooth-flow obstruction may be recorded analytically, but no separate dynamics experiment will be added.
