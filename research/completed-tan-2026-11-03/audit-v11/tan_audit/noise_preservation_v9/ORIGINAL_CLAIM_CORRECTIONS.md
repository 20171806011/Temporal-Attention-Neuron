## Interpretive wording to narrow, not factual result reversals

- **Selective filtering and edge preservation.** The single-seed figure description claims precise transition spikes and rare noisy-period firing ([experiments.tex:26](../../Temporal-Attention-Neuron/snapshot/manuscript/sections/experiments.tex#L26), [33](../../Temporal-Attention-Neuron/snapshot/manuscript/sections/experiments.tex#L33)); broader wording calls TAN a behaviorally relevant temporal edge detector ([39](../../Temporal-Attention-Neuron/snapshot/manuscript/sections/experiments.tex#L39)). The original numerical endpoint establishes reduced spiking in its scored interval, not preserved detection or selectivity. The frozen control asks the narrower known-onset step-presence question. A positive result would not establish clean-waveform reconstruction or general denoising; a negative result would not establish absence of all information in the trace.
- **Why a lower GRU score occurs.** The claim that GRU's lower Task B rate demonstrates successful distillation ([experiments.tex:239](../../Temporal-Attention-Neuron/snapshot/manuscript/sections/experiments.tex#L239)) is not established by that rate. Reduced activity and faithful imitation require different evidence. Preserve the recorded comparison; describe the proposed explanation as untested by this endpoint.
- **Ablation “improvement” versus “MILD-DEG.”** At [experiments.tex:301](../../Temporal-Attention-Neuron/snapshot/manuscript/sections/experiments.tex#L301), a lower no-attention rate is reinterpreted as mild degradation without a companion signal-preservation score. The rate establishes the stated direction of activity change; it cannot adjudicate whether the change preserves useful responses. Retain the historical label and separate the limitation, including the branch mismatch above.
- **Energy.** “Energetically wasteful” in [experiments.tex:24](../../Temporal-Attention-Neuron/snapshot/manuscript/sections/experiments.tex#L24) exceeds the measurement. The paper already states that net energy advantage is unestablished because TAN adds history storage and attention computation ([28](../../Temporal-Attention-Neuron/snapshot/manuscript/sections/experiments.tex#L28)); preserve that caveat. Discrete current/state sums and observed spike counts are proxies, not ATP, joules, matched physical gain, or total energy. Thinned LIF still generates and computes its original spikes.

Suggested short replacement: “With the recorded Task B parameters, TAN has a lower late-plateau spike rate than LIF. The historical field is named `false_spike_rate`, but the scorer does not distinguish noise-induced spikes from deterministic responses. Signal preservation requires a separate diagnostic. Module-ablation results use a rectified-surprise variant and must be interpreted within that branch. No net physical energy advantage was measured.”

This wording should be supplemented by the new control's completed results only after they are independently validated; no anticipated outcome is asserted here.
