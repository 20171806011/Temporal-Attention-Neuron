The present proof writes the Newton recurrence explicitly rather than claiming to invent it.
* M. Vetterli, P. Marziliano, T. Blu, *Sampling Signals with Finite Rate of Innovation*, IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing 50(6), 1417–1428 (2002). Author-institution publication page: https://bigwww.epfl.ch/publications/vetterli0201.html . This is classical annihilating/locator-filter background, not a claim that the paper proves this exact protocol or its safety budget.
