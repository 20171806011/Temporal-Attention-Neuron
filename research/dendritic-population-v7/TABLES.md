# V7 complete per-seed tables

All tabulated outcomes and intervals passed the separately written native/data/optimizer/statistics checker. The checks are internal to this workflow, not external independent replication.

All 160 trained models are included on both final training and held-out data. Each of the twenty seed blocks has 128 training and 512 test episodes shared by eight conditions with the same initial weights. There is no model selection or exclusion. Accuracy and class recall count abstention as error. The principal uncertainty unit is twenty paired training/data blocks, not 160 independent models or individual episodes. Raw numerical precision, full signed-count-margin histograms and native arrays are retained in `results/TRAIN_LOCK.json`, `results/TEST_SUMMARY.json` and the per-model archives.
