### 1. The spike-input contract has an outcome-relevant off-by-one conflict

[§5.2](C:/Users/李则徐/Downloads/TAN_Review/P0_20260917_181757/p1_experiments/p1b/P1B_PRE_REGISTRATION_PROTOCOL_V2_5.md:124) supplies \(s_1,\ldots,s_{100}\) to the decoders but identifies **step 101** as the excluded 10 ms endpoint.

Under the registered recurrence and end-of-step timestamp convention,

\[
t(s_k)=T_{\mathrm{cue}}+kh.
\]

Therefore:

- \(s_{99}\) is timestamped at **9.9 ms**: eligible.
- \(s_{100}\) is timestamped at **10.0 ms**: excluded.
- Step 101 would be at **10.1 ms**, outside the 100-step cue simulation.

This conflicts with §3.1, §8.3, and the step numbering used by the refractory fixture.

It can change results. For a legal zero-membrane encoding obtained with \(V_i=-K_i\), choose \(q=1.58\). Independent evaluation gives

\[
u_{99}=0.9958271725<1,\qquad
u_{\mathrm{cand},99}=1.0016689008>1.
\]

The first spike occurs at step 100, exactly on the excluded deadline. The correct window observation is **silence/abstention**; including all 100 raw bits instead produces count 1.

**Required correction:** retain the 100 raw integration outputs if desired, but explicitly define decoder eligibility by the registered timestamp rule. With this indexing, only \(s_1,\ldots,s_{99}\) can contribute; \(s_{100}\) must be excluded from both count and latency decoding. Update G1.6b consistently.

A related numerical-label correction belongs in [§8.2](C:/Users/李则徐/Downloads/TAN_Review/P0_20260917_181757/p1_experiments/p1b/P1B_PRE_REGISTRATION_PROTOCOL_V2_5.md:241): the refractory state is \(r_{99}=0.2\) ms **before** the final update and \(r_{100}=0.1\) ms **after** it. The required \(u_{\mathrm{readout}}=0\) conclusion remains correct.

### 2. G1.3a’s sampling row does not define the joint observation its null requires

The [sampling table](C:/Users/李则徐/Downloads/TAN_Review/P0_20260917_181757/p1_experiments/p1b/P1B_PRE_REGISTRATION_PROTOCOL_V2_5.md:83) specifies **“1 (Fixed query)”** and 1,000 queries for each leakage test. But \(167/288\) and \(62/243\) are the registered **joint two-role delivery probabilities** for the fixed-order, distinct-candidate control.

For comparison, under the frozen uniformly selected-target generator, the corresponding single-role probability for \(N=3\) is

\[
\frac13+\frac23P(|V_i-V_j|<0.5)
=\frac13+\frac23\frac{23}{144}
=\frac{95}{216}\approx0.439815,
\]

not \(62/243\approx0.255144\). The frozen control distinguishes its two output channels explicitly. [Control implementation](C:/Users/李则徐/Downloads/TAN_Review/P0_20260917_181757/p1_experiments/models.py:123)

**Required correction:** specify that every leakage-test history retains the randomly selected distinct targets A/B, obtains both fixed-order control predictions, and contributes **one joint-success Bernoulli observation**. The Wilson denominator remains 1,000 independent histories—not 2,000 independent probes.

One control call returning both outputs is acceptable; if that is intended, label it as one **paired-output call**, rather than one query/probe. Otherwise, register two role predictions per history and 2,000 role predictions per \(N\).

Also freeze the \(N=2/N=3\) allocation of the 1,000 oracle histories; its current row specifies only their combined total.
