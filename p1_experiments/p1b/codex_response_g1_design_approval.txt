### 1. The deadline readout is undefined during refractoriness

Your selected pre-reset observation time is acceptable. The remaining problem is that [§2.2](C:/Users/李则徐/Downloads/TAN_Review/P0_20260917_181757/p1_experiments/p1b/P1B_PRE_REGISTRATION_PROTOCOL_V2_3.md:37) defines `u_cand` only when \(r_n=0\), while [§5.1](C:/Users/李则徐/Downloads/TAN_Review/P0_20260917_181757/p1_experiments/p1b/P1B_PRE_REGISTRATION_PROTOCOL_V2_3.md:95) requires it unconditionally at the final cue step.

This is reachable under legal inputs:

- Choose event values \(V_i=-K_i\). Encoding then leaves \(u=0,r=0\).
- For a requested key \(q=1.8\), before the first spike,
  \[
  u_k=1.8(1-0.99^k).
  \]
- \(u_{80}=0.9944582<1\), while \(u_{81}=1.0025136>1\): the spike occurs at 8.1 ms.
- Only 19 steps remain before the deadline. The neuron is still refractory at 10 ms, and the final step does not calculate `u_cand`.

**Required amendment:** make the readout a total function. A physically consistent convention would be:

```text
u_readout = u_reset if r_before_final_step > 0 else u_cand_final_step
```

Explicitly freeze that convention—or another justified observable—and add this reachable case to G1. A stale candidate from the preceding spike must not silently become the deadline observation.

### 2. The baseline prerequisite for equivalence interpretation has disappeared

[v2.2 §7.3](C:/Users/李则徐/Downloads/TAN_Review/P0_20260917_181757/p1_experiments/p1b/P1B_PRE_REGISTRATION_PROTOCOL_V2_2.md:153) explicitly halted equivalence evaluation when M1 failed its baseline. [v2.3 §7.3–7.4](C:/Users/李则徐/Downloads/TAN_Review/P0_20260917_181757/p1_experiments/p1b/P1B_PRE_REGISTRATION_PROTOCOL_V2_3.md:155) registers insufficiency but no longer specifies that prerequisite.

Why this matters: if both models fail every one of 1,000 paired trials, the specified Newcombe procedure gives approximately

\[
CI_{90\%}(d)=[-0.002698243,\,+0.002698243],
\]

which passes the \(\pm0.005\) equivalence criterion.

That is not a defect in the interval formula. It demonstrates that equivalent delivery scores can occur when neither system delivers anything successfully.

**Required amendment:** either restore the baseline prerequisite for the confirmatory ablation conclusion, or explicitly preregister below-floor comparisons as **floor-limited comparisons**, without inferring preservation or redundancy of a functioning memory mechanism. State the decision separately for the relevant \(N\)/delay conditions.

Keep `M1_CARRIER_INSUFFICIENT`. Also retain the distinction between a population prediction and a deterministic assertion about every finite sample.
